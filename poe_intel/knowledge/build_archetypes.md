# PoE 3.28 Mirage — Build Archetypes & League Starters

## S-Tier League Starters (proven, cheap, reliable)

### Blight of Contagion Trickster (by Ky)
- Playstyle: DoT spread caster — apply, run, everything dies
- Budget: very cheap start, scales with investment
- Pros: insane clear, defensive Trickster, minimal gear needed
- Cons: boss single target can feel slow early

### Sunder Ignite Elementalist (by Greek)
- Playstyle: slam + ignite, huge HP pool
- Budget: cheap
- Pros: very tanky, comfortable mapper, big AoE
- Cons: melee-ish, slower clear than pure casters

### Toxic Rain Pathfinder (by PathofEvening)
- Playstyle: DoT bow, drop pods and move
- Budget: dirt cheap
- Pros: league-start king for years, great survivability, Pathfinder flasks
- Cons: DoT playstyle not for everyone, ceiling is limited

### Pyroclast Mine Saboteur (by PathofEvening)
- Playstyle: mine placer, detonate for massive damage
- Budget: cheap
- Pros: ridiculous single target, Saboteur defenses (blind, regen)
- Cons: mine playstyle is love-or-hate, can feel clunky

## A-Tier Starters (strong, slightly more investment)

### Volatile Dead Spellslinger Elementalist (by Ky)
- Playstyle: auto-cast VD through spellslinger while attacking
- Budget: low-medium
- Pros: smooth gameplay loop, good clear + single target
- Cons: needs specific gear setup (spellslinger links)

### Poison SRS Necromancer (by BalorMage)
- Playstyle: summon skulls, they poison everything
- Budget: cheap
- Pros: minion AI does the work, scales well into maps
- Cons: minion build = can feel indirect

### Dominating Blow Guardian (by GhazzyTV)
- Playstyle: hit enemies -> they become your army
- BUFFED in 3.28: summons on rare hits + increased durations
- Guardian: new Marshal of Divinity + permanent Sentinel
- Budget: low-medium
- Pros: very tanky, army grows as you map, Guardian reworked positively
- Cons: needs initial hit to build army, slow ramp in boss fights

### Siege Ballista Hierophant (by POEGuy)
- Playstyle: place ballista totems, they snipe everything
- Budget: low
- Pros: safe (you run, totems kill), Hiero mana/defense
- Cons: totem placement speed nerfed (0.5s base for ranged), may feel slower

## B-Tier (viable but niche or higher investment)

### Boneshatter Juggernaut
- Melee slam, self-damage for trauma stacks
- Jugg = unkillable, but clear speed is mediocre

### Lacerate Gladiator
- Bleed gladiator, solid all-around
- Less popular this league, no specific buffs

### Cyclone Slayer
- Spin to win, Slayer overleech
- Needs weapon investment for damage

### Spectre Summoner Necromancer (by GhazzyTV)
- NERFED: spectres lost hidden ele damage bonus on phys conversion
- Still viable but weaker than before

## Builds Affected by 3.28 Nerfs (AVOID or proceed with caution)
- **ES Stackers**: INT->ES halved (per 10 instead of per 5)
- **Earthshatter builds**: damage effectiveness 393% -> 329%
- **Warcry Slammers**: Autoexertion/Overexertion nerfed
- **Energy Blade**: quality bonus changed
- **Spectre-based**: hidden conversion bonus removed

## Builds Benefiting from 3.28 Buffs (CONSIDER)
- **Brand builds**: Penance Brand +10%, Storm Brand buffed
- **Absolution**: +40% damage, way longer duration
- **Dominating Blow Guardian**: summon on rares + Guardian rework
- **Holy Flame Totem**: 3-6 projectiles
- **Consecrated Path**: damage effectiveness up

## Defense Layers Checklist
1. Capped ele resists (75/75/75, overcap by 20+ for Ele Weakness maps)
2. Chaos res >= 0 (aim 75 for HC)
3. Life pool >= 4000+ (5500+ recommended, 6500+ HC)
4. At least 2 of: Armour, Evasion, Block, Spell Suppression
5. Recovery: leech, regen, recoup, or flask sustain
6. Ailment immunity (freeze, shock, ignite)
7. Stun immunity or high threshold
8. Phys damage reduction (Endurance charges, Armour, Fortify)

## League Start Economy Tips
- Day 1-2: sell EVERYTHING, hoard chaos/divine
- Don't buy gear until day 3+ (prices crash fast)
- Farm Djinn mechanic — new league = new loot table = profit
- Awakened gems removed from drop pool = existing ones very expensive
- Map fast > map perfect — speed is currency at league start
