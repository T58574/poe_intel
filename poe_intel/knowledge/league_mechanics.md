# League 3.28 — Mirage

## League Mechanic: Djinn & Wishes
- Encounter trapped Djinn in maps
- Defeat physical manifestations to enter **The Astral Realm**
- In Astral Realm: select 1 of 3 **Wishes** that modify the instance
- Wishes enhance monster density, drops, and loot quality
- Fight through enhanced zone to reach the tether and free the Djinn
- Higher tier maps = stronger Djinn = better wishes

## Major Skill Buffs
- **Penance Brand**: +10% base damage, improved Added Damage effectiveness
- **Storm Brand**: significant damage increase across all gem levels
- **Consecrated Path**: enhanced base + added damage effectiveness
- **Holy Flame Totem**: now fires 3-6 projectiles (was static 3)
- **Absolution**: +40% damage, duration 10s -> 15s, debuff 3s
- **Dominating Blow**: summons sentinels on hitting rares, increased durations
- **Animate Guardian of Smiting**: 0-76% more damage (was 0-38%), larger AoE
- **Holy Slam** (HoP/DomBlow minions): +32% AoE

## Major Skill Nerfs
- **Earthshatter**: damage effectiveness 393% -> 329%
- **Detonate Dead of Scavenging**: lowered ailment damage multiplier
- **Static Strike**: beam damage -10%
- **Energy Blade**: quality now gives attack speed, not ES
- **Intelligence**: grants 1% max ES per 10 int (was per 5 int) — huge nerf to ES stackers
- **Autoexertion**: mana reservation 15% -> 20%
- **Overexertion**: additional damage per warcry 12-18% -> 7-10%
- **Awakened Support Gems**: all removed except Empower/Enlighten/Enhance
- **Raised Spectres**: no longer get hidden more elemental damage bonus on phys conversion

## Passive Tree Changes
- Two new clusters near Templar: accuracy/Consecrated Ground and accuracy/Area
- New Protection Mastery (replaces Wither effect reduction)
- Fire Mastery reworked: includes Ignite Duration bonuses
- INT -> ES scaling halved (see nerfs above)

## Ascendancy Changes
- **Chieftain**: Hinekora explosion chance adjusted
- **Guardian**: new Marshal of Divinity notable, Sentinel permanent (no duration)
- **Warden**: Lesson of the Seasons = flat damage reduction per Bark
- **Assassin/Saboteur**: updated crit and cooldown mechanics

## Atlas Overhaul
- Maps no longer tied to specific areas
- **Astrolabes** create "Shaped Regions" affecting multiple maps
- **Memory Threads** via Memory of Loneliness, Trauma, Reverence items
- T17 maps become **Nightmare Maps**
- Voidstone acquisition restructured across Incarnation bosses

## Economy Predictions
- League start: divine typically 100-150c
- Week 1: prices volatile, farm currency not items
- Week 2+: economy stabilizes, time to buy gear
- Endgame uniques (Mageblood, HH): cheapest around week 3-4
- Awakened gems removed = existing ones will spike in price

## Atlas Strategy
- Rush T16s, engage Djinn mechanic in every map for extra loot
- Astrolabe system = more control over map farming
- Nightmare Maps (T17) = new endgame challenge tier
